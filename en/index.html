Frequently asked questions

Do I have to book several packages, or can I take a single service?

Single services are always possible. The packages — for example Soft Landing — are just suggestions from the toolkit and can be shortened, extended or combined. Individual lessons, a training plan or nutrition coaching are equally bookable.

For companies: how does this pay off?

Experience shows that a failed international assignment costs a company a multiple of the integration programme — through re-recruitment, vacancy and lost productivity. The programme is therefore risk mitigation, not a training-cost line item. Terms on request.

Who is the Soft Landing programme suitable for?

For people who are new to Switzerland and want to arrive professionally — from day one. Also suitable for partners.

Do I have to be on site?

No. Live sessions take place by video, and you use the learning platform independently of time. On-site appointments in German-speaking Switzerland are possible on request, for example for application or authority support.

Do you also offer nutrition coaching?

Yes — as coaching on habits, everyday practicality and sports nutrition. Not nutrition therapy, not medical treatment and no health-insurance billing. If someone needs medical nutrition, I refer them to a recognised specialist.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within two weeks of the intro call. For urgent cases (start date already agreed) short-notice start dates are possible too.
